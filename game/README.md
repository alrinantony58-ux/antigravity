# TEMPLE SURF 3D — Endless Runner 🏃⚡

> An exhilarating 3D browser endless runner blending the best of **Subway Surfers** and **Temple Run**! Run across endless cyber subway rails and ancient Aztec ruins, dodge oncoming trains, leap over electric hurdles, slide under laser beams, collect gold coins, and unlock legendary runners.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 🖥️ Keyboard Controls (Desktop / Laptop):
- **Move Left:** `Left Arrow` or `A`
- **Move Right:** `Right Arrow` or `D`
- **Jump:** `Up Arrow`, `W`, or `Spacebar` (Leap over low hurdles and gaps)
- **Slide / Duck:** `Down Arrow` or `S` (Slide under high overhead signs and laser beams; also fast-drops from jumps)
- **Pause / Resume:** `P` or `Escape`

### 📱 Touch & Mobile Controls:
- **Swipe Gestures:**
  - Swipe Left / Right to switch lanes
  - Swipe Up to Jump
  - Swipe Down to Slide / Fast-drop
- **Virtual Touch Pad:** On-screen action buttons at the bottom of the screen.

## 🛠️ Technical Highlights

- **Full 3D WebGL Engine:** Built with Three.js (r128) featuring real-time directional soft shadows, fog depth, dynamic camera banking, and FOV expansion on speed-boost.
- **Procedural Web Audio API:** 100% self-contained synthesized retro-synth background beat and sound effects (jump whoosh, slide dash, coin chime, crash impact) with zero external MP3 file lag or network blocks.
- **Buttery Smooth 60 FPS:** Dynamic segment recycling system and lightweight procedural geometry.
- **Zero Build-Step Required:** Double-click `game/index.html` to play instantly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox!
